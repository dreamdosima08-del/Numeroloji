✦ Frequently Asked Questions ✦

What is numerology?

Numerology is an ancient system that studies the influence of numbers on personality, destiny and life events. Numbers calculated from your birth date and name reveal your core character and life purpose.

How is the life path number calculated?

The life path number is found by adding all digits of your birth date and reducing to a single digit. For example, 03/15/1990 gives 0+3+1+5+1+9+9+0=28, 2+8=10, 1+0=1. The life path number is 1.

Is the numerology analysis free?

Yes. On Numerolojim.com.tr, life path number, soul urge, personality and love compatibility analyses are completely free. Just enter your name and birth date; results are calculated instantly.

What are master numbers (11, 22, 33)?

Master numbers are special numbers in numerology that are not reduced to a single digit. 11 represents intuition, 22 the power of great building, and 33 unconditional love and teaching. They carry extraordinary potential.
